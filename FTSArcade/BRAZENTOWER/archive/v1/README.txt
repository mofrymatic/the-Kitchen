BRAZEN TOWER 0.5.0

brazen-0.5.0.html is fully standalone: the curated Brazen Tower world and HUD atlas art is embedded as data URIs.
The two PNG files are included separately for inspection and future atlas work.

Major changes:
- Expanded U-shaped wraparound mezzanine overlooking the lobby.
- Symmetric twin 8-step staircases to Level 02.
- Grand central medallion floor and suspended chandelier sightline.
- Ground-level Brazen Theaters and FTS Arcade side destinations.
- Authored atlas furniture, signage, elevator, lounge, planter, lighting, and decorative sprites replace procedural block props.
- Authored HUD atlas crops and pistol frames embedded.
- 33 convex sectors / 108 linedefs; map validation passes.
