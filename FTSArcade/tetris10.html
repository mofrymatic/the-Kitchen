HOW TO PLAY

Stack tetrominoes into complete horizontal lines. Keep the well open, chain difficult clears, and use the selected rotation system deliberately.

  • ← → Move · ↓ Soft drop · SPACE Hard drop
  • ↑ / X Rotate clockwise · Z Counter-clockwise · A 180°
  • C / SHIFT Hold · P / ESC Pause · O / S Settings
  • SRS supports modern wall/floor kicks. ARS is tighter, right-biased, and favors TGM-style placement discipline.
